Deskman

Deskman is your home assistant. Talk with him, set reminders, and make video calls.

Talking to Deskman

  • Say "robot" or "deskman", then your request: "robot, what time is it?"
  • After a reply you can keep talking for 20 seconds without the name.
  • He cannot hear you while he is speaking, wait for him to finish.
  • Say "be quiet" to put him back to idle.

Modes

  • Local: Runs on the robot, no internet needed.
  • Cloud: OpenAI for answers, faster and smarter.
  • Realtime: OpenAI hears and speaks directly, most natural.

What he can do

  • Watch: His eyes follow your face using the camera, and his head follows you when he is listening.
  • Look: Say "look left", "look right", "look up", "look down", and "look center" to move his head.
  • Time: Say "what time is it?", or "how many days until December 15".
  • Volume: Say "volume 80" to set a percent of volume.
  • Remember: Say "remember my name is Tom", and he will remember your name over power restarts.
  • Remind: Say "remind me at 10 PM for bedtime" for a daily spoken reminder.
  • Videos: Say "record a video", or "play a video".
  • Status: Say "how is your battery?", or "what system are you running on?"
  • Calls: Call robot to robot, or from a web browser, at https://teleportconnect.com/teleport
  • Calendar: Connect your calendar using the tool script.

The touch menu

Tap the button on the right of the status bar. The mode row (Local, Cloud, Realtime) shows along the bottom.
ListenTurn voice listening on or off.
CameraShow or hide the camera preview with face tracking.
RecordStart recording a video, tap Stop to finish.
VideosPlay or delete saved recordings.
CallStart a video call.
SettingsOpens a column to the left with Demo, Move, Audio, Volume, WiFi, and Exit.
DemoStays listening, looks around every 30 seconds, volume 80%, goes green.
MoveSweeps the servos.
AudioMicrophone and speaker test.

Get on WiFi

  1. Tap the menu, then Settings, then WiFi.
  2. Tap your network in the list.
  3. Type the password and confirm.
  4. Top line shows WiFi: name when connected.
Local needs no WiFi. Cloud and Realtime need internet.

Connect by SSH

Address is at the top of the WiFi screen:
ssh deskman@deskman4.local
Password: deskman
  • Use your robot's name instead of deskman4.
  • Cursor or Claude Code: use the same address.

Handy commands

tail -f robot/log.txtShow the log on screen
robot/restart_services.shRestart the robot and video call services, no password
cd talk && ./talk.pyRun the voice program by hand
sudo service robot stopStop the robot first if one is running
