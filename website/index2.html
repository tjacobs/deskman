Natural voice

Just talk to it — and it talks right back.

Deskman uses OpenAI's Realtime voice, so conversation feels instant and natural. No wake-word gymnastics, no menus, no phone app. Say something, and Deskman answers out loud in real time.

Face to face, wherever you are

Deskman makes video calls — call another Deskman across the house or across the country, or hop on from any web browser. Great for keeping up with family, checking in on grandparents, or bringing a far-away friend to the table.

Looks at you

Deskman turns its head to face you and nods along as you chat. Its little hat lifts to reveal the camera when it's time to see and talk.

Sees you

A built-in camera tracks your face, so Deskman keeps looking your way as you move around — and it's the same camera you'll use for video calls.
